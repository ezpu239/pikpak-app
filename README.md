**What Is PikPak App and How Does It Work?**

PikPak markets itself as a "private cloud drive," which is technically accurate but undersells the interesting part. Most cloud storage services (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) are glorified hard drives in the sky—you upload files from your device, they sit there, you download them back when needed. That's it.

PikPak does something different. Its core feature is **server-side cloud download**. You give it a link—a torrent magnet link, a URL, a Telegram file, a link from X (formerly Twitter)—and PikPak's servers go get that file for you. No local download client. No leaving your laptop running. No ISP throttling. The download happens on PikPak's side, at PikPak's speed, and the result lands in your private 10TB storage.

More than 80% of files complete in seconds. A 20GB video that would take hours on a home connection gets pulled into PikPak in minutes because PikPak's servers have bandwidth your ISP doesn't give you.

After that, the file sits in your cloud drive and you can:
- Stream it in up to 4K original quality directly in the app—no compression, no re-encoding
- Download it to any device at whatever speed your local internet allows
- Share it, organize it in folders, or extract compressed archives directly in the cloud

The privacy angle matters too. Because the downloading happens on PikPak's servers, your ISP only sees traffic between you and PikPak, not what you're actually saving.
